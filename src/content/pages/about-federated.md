Federated Auto Parts is a program built around independently owned and operated auto parts distributors.

## Our focus

We help member stores compete with national chains by combining:

- Strong manufacturer relationships
- Marketing and training programs
- Local inventory and delivery expertise

## Why independents

Customers trust the counter person who knows their shop. Federated exists to keep that local advantage while giving members the tools of a larger network.
