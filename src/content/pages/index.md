#### Our Mission

Federated Auto Parts Distributors is fueled by exemplary vision and leadership and driven by aggressive service and top quality name brand parts. Founded in 1985 by Art Fisher, Federated provides a vast array of marketing programs designed to meet the needs of all of our members and their diverse trading areas. Federated strongly encourages the utilization of aggressive national advertising and promotional programs which have been specifically designed to tie both dealer and installer customers to the Federated jobber.

The primary goal of Federated Auto Parts has always been to exceed customer expectation. To meet this goal Federated continues to supply the highest quality parts from today’s most respected suppliers while at the same time maintaining the loyalty of the best professional service dealers.

#### Our History

Federated Auto Parts was founded in 1985 and is headquartered in Staunton, Virginia. The goal of the cooperative was to pool resources and purchasing power to better compete with large, national chains.

Over the next few decades, Federated Auto Parts grew rapidly through acquisitions and partnerships. In 1998, the company merged with the Automotive Distribution Network, creating one of the largest automotive parts distributors in North America.
