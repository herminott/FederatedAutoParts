This privacy notice applies to the Federated Auto Parts marketing and news website.

## Information we collect

This static site does not require an account and does not operate a server-side user database. Standard web server and hosting logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) may be collected by our hosting provider for security, reliability, and analytics.

## Cookies and third parties

Embedded third-party content (for example maps, video, or fonts loaded from Google Fonts) may set cookies or collect usage data according to those providers’ policies.

## Links to other sites

Our pages may link to Federated Car Care, Federated Link, The Group Training Academy, and other partner sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

## Contact

For privacy questions about this site, contact Federated Auto Parts headquarters. See the [Federated Headquarters](/federated-headquarters/) page for current contact information.

## Updates

We may update this notice as the site evolves. The “last updated” date will change when material revisions are published.
