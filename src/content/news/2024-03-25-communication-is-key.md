As many of you already know, the 2024 Group National Meeting, which was scheduled for April 14-17 at the Mirage in Las Vegas, will be moved to a different date which has not yet been determined.

It would have been great if we could have found out a little bit sooner than January that we would have to move an event which involves approximately 1,000 attendees. It is next to impossible to find a venue at this late date that could accommodate our group with both sleeping rooms and meeting space. We are currently working on a Plan B and hope to have something to report on very soon.

In the meantime, to keep Federated members informed in all areas, Federated will be hosting a quarterly Zoom meeting. Our first quarterly meeting is scheduled to take place on March 21 at 1:00 p.m. ET. The quarterly Zoom meeting will feature updates in purchasing, marketing, the supply chain, and the industry.  The Zoom call will also feature a supplier. Our goal is to improve communication by giving members the opportunity to ask questions, promote collaboration, creativity and innovation. The Zoom meeting provides an excellent opportunity to have other members of your team involved in the meeting.

Federated members are always very engaged and the ability to share ideas and experiences with other members has made our group one of the most successful groups in the industry.

Mark your calendars for March 21 at 1:00 p.m. ET. A meeting agenda, along with a Zoom link, will be sent to you shortly. I look forward to seeing everyone soon.
