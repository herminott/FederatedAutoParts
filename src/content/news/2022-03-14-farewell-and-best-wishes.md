On Friday, March 4, the Federated staff held a retirement celebration for one of its co-workers, Donna Ergenbright.

For the past 20 years, Donna has been the accounts payable manager for the Co-Man Warehouse. When Donna came to work at the Co-Man Warehouse in 2002, Co-Man had 10 employees and 16 product lines. Today, Co-Man has 79 product lines and 49 employees.

Donna has seen many changes over the years. Co-Man has grown and is now one of the largest suppliers for its Federated members.

We asked Donna what her plans are now that she is retired. Donna is an avid gardener and is looking forward to planting her garden for the upcoming season. She is also looking forward to spending more time with her dog and her grandchildren.

We appreciate the hard work and dedication that Donna has given to the Co-Man Warehouse over the years, and while we are sad to see Donna leave, we are all excited for her and wish her the very best as she begins the next chapter of her life.
