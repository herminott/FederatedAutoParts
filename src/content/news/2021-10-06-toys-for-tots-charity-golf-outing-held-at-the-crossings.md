The Federated 400 NASCAR race is always one of the highlights of the year at Federated. This year’s event was no exception and the Sept. 11 date made it an even more meaningful event in that it presented Federated with the opportunity to salute First Responders and honor those who made the ultimate sacrifice on that day 20 years ago.

Another highlight of the year at Federated is the annual Toys for Tots charity golf tournament. We are happy to report that this year’s tournament raised approximately $51,000 for Toys for Tots. We view this event as the kick-off to the toy collection campaign for Toys for Tots, which begins in October, and lasts until mid-to-late December. (Toy distribution also takes place during mid-to-late December).

Toys for Tots has coordinators who organize, coordinate, and manage the campaign, but the ultimate success of the program depends on the support of each local community and the generosity of the people who donate toys.

As business leaders, please consider allowing coordinators to place drop-off/collection boxes in your stores. Encourage your employees to become involved in the toy collection process and please consider making a monetary contribution.

Contributions to Toys for Tots can be mailed to the following address:  
  
**Marine Toys for Tots Foundation**  
Gift Processing Administrator  
18251 Quantico Gateway Drive  
Triangle, VA 22172

You can also donate online at: [www.toysfortots.org/donate/](https://www.toysfortots.org/donate/)
