A baseball game at Busch Stadium, an incredible World of Outlaws race and a “Get Dirty with Kenny” driving experience were just some of the highlights that the grand prize winners in the Federated Suite-Stakes contest enjoyed during their ultimate weekend in St. Louis earlier this month.

“We want to thank everyone who participated in the Federated Suite-Stakes contest and congratulate our grand prize winners who we know from the feedback had a once-in-a-lifetime experience,” said J.R. Bishop, director of event marketing and member services for Federated. “We set a record for the amount of people who entered the Suite-Stakes promotion, and we awarded more prizes than ever before.”

The grand prize winners of the Ultimate Weekend and their guests included: Savanna Emery and Michael Federico, Randall Ferguson and Willian Marstellar, Adena and Howard Fetzer, Anthony Inglis and Jellis Anthony, Jennifer and Jeremy Johnson, Lisa and Andre Lemieux, Chris and William Ready and Jeffery and Derrick Renner.

The Ultimate Weekend started off with a St. Louis Cardinals-Colorado Rockies game from a skybox at Busch Stadium. The next day, the Suite-Stakes winners attended the Federated Ironman 55 World of Outlaws race at the Federated Auto Parts Raceway in Pevely, Missouri. At the track, they were entertained with one of the best World of Outlaws finishes of the season as Kyle Larson edged out Federated sponsored driver Logan Schuchart, who was driving the Federated World of Outlaws sprint car.

The next day, the contest winners returned to the track to enjoy a “Get Dirty with Kenny” driving experience with Federated spokesperson and dirt car legend Kenny Schrader.

The Federated Suite-Stakes promotion was conducted for Federated customers in good standing who enrolled in the contest before June 30, 2023. Thousands of prizes were awarded this year, including cruises, trips to the 2024 DirtCar Nationals in Florida and customer training weekends at The Group Training Academy Training and Research Center in Florida. “With the Federated Suite-Stakes program, we have developed an annual program that can help Federated members, customers and vendors grow their businesses at a whole new level,” said Bishop. “We also have the opportunity to thank our customers with thousands of great prizes. We are already working on the 2024 contest and expect it will be bigger and better than ever before.”
