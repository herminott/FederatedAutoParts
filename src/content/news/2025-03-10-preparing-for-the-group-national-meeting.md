Preparations are in full swing for the upcoming The Group National Meeting, which will be held April 28-May 1, 2025, at the Gaylord National Resort and Conference Center in National Harbor, Maryland.

Hopefully, you have already registered for the meeting and made your reservations at the Gaylord. You will need to be registered in order to receive the information that will be coming to you soon regarding your one-on-one meetings.

Obviously, your one-on-one meetings with vendors will be a very valuable part of the meeting and there are things that you can do that will help you be prepared to meet with vendors. Below are just a few things that can help make your one-on-one meetings more productive.

- Perform a thorough review of the program posted on GroupNet for each vendor that you are meeting with.
- Use the information that Al Joyner can provide for you from the data warehouse.
- Bring enough people from your organization to adequately cover all meetings and issues. You might consider bringing people who specialize in different areas (i.e., purchasing, marketing, sales etc.).
- Be prepared to discuss any issues that you currently have with a particular vendor.
- Ask the vendor what they can do to help grow your sales.
- Ask about training opportunities.

These are just a few things that we suggest, and I am sure that you will have several more to add to this list.

We are very fortunate to have several talented and dedicated staff members at Federated, the Alliance and the Pronto Network, who work very hard to make sure that you get the most value out of your time spent at The Group National Meeting. Thanks to these individuals for all the hard work and effort that goes into this meeting.

I look forward to seeing everyone at the end of April!
