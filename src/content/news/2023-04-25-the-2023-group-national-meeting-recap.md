If you did not attend the 2023 The Group National Meeting last month, you really missed a great opportunity to reconnect with other members, manufacturers, and the industry.  Federated was especially excited to have our new joint venture partner, the Alliance attend this meeting. Including the Federated Alliance, the Pronto Network, and our vendor partners, there were over a 1,000 attendees for this four-day event.

The meeting kicked off on Monday night with a timely keynote address from Mark Scharenbroich, author of the book “Nice Bike.” Mark’s message was fun and well-delivered. It was all about how we connect with other people. We connect every day with our family, co-workers, and manufacturer partners. It is important how we make those connections, and we need to make sure in our everyday lives that we give everyone we connect with that 13th donut that Mark spoke about.

Following the keynote address, we marched to the trade show floor, led by a colonial drum and fife corps. Bosch, the first “Elite” supplier was given the honor of marching at the front of the group. At the booth show, we not only enjoyed a nice reception, but we also had the opportunity to visit with our vendor partners and other members. Again, it was all about making those connections.

Tuesday was a busy day with one-on-one meetings being held in conjunction with the booth show. This format works well and members and vendors can see everyone that they would need or want to see.

Tuesday evening everyone was invited to a private reception and dinner at the National Museum of American History. It was a great opportunity after a long day of meetings to relax and enjoy the three floors of exhibits along with great food and drinks.

Wednesday, the booth show continued until noon and in the afternoon each group had their own individual breakout sessions. Wednesday evening we had an awards ceremony to honor the members and vendors who had done an outstanding job. It is always important to recognize and celebrate those who have gone above and beyond.

On Thursday morning, there was a general session for all members. We had some very informative industry presentations as well as an outlook on the aftermarket and economy from Simeon Gutman with Morgan Stanley.

Those are just some of the highlights from the week. There was so much information to be gained at The Group National meeting it was a great way to connect. We are already looking forward to the meeting in 2024, which will be held April 14-19 at the Mirage in Las Vegas. Be sure to mark your calendars and we hope you will make every effort to attend.
