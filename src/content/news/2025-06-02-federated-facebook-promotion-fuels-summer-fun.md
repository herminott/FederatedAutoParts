Looking for a way to help “fuel” your summer activities? Enter the Federated “Fuel Your Summer Fun” contest on the [Federated Auto Parts](https://woobox.com/syd8da?fbclid=IwY2xjawKqtJxleHRuA2FlbQIxMABicmlkETE3WWtFT0I2WGprRjhXZk4xAR5vCc4Z-uDhNSD4E1YxxnlS1pP8jBV8-au0j4Pbm6iJCyZ-UOAAcdrOMxG_zQ_aem_ztuc33hoSguok4345TBuQQ) and [Federated Car Care](https://woobox.com/upjccf?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTAAYnJpZBExN1lrRU9CNlhqa0Y4V2ZOMQEexHUxWlFaLOU4uPT5dMg2e2SPl6FO5shxIEmftHth56kGgyCZo5PaCX9Kqu8_aem_gwB1vqh5FKGqmyluC6p39Q) Facebook pages and you could be a winner.

“At Federated, we like to add a little extra fun to summer by giving our Facebook friends a chance to win in the Federated ‘Fuel Your Summer Fun’ contest,” said John Marcum, director of marketing at Federated Auto Parts. “Through the end of June, we will announce four $50 gift card winners every Friday. Then, to add some extra fireworks to the contest, we will announce one grand prize winner of a $500 gift card on July 4.”

To enter, visit Federated on Facebook, click on the special Fuel Your Summer Fun contest link and complete the entry form. Each Friday through June 28, Federated will announce four randomly selected winners of a $50 gift card on its [Federated Auto Parts](https://www.facebook.com/FederatedAP) and [Federated Car Care](https://www.facebook.com/FederatedCarCare) Facebook platforms. On Friday, July 4, one grand prize winner of a $500 gift card will be announced. The deadline to enter is June 22.
