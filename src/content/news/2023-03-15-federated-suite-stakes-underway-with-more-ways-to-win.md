This year’s Federated Suite-Stakes contest is the largest one yet with bigger prizes, more chances to win, and sensational grand prizes, including an ocean cruise and racing weekend extravaganzas.

“Federated Suite-Stakes has become an exciting annual program. In the past two years, we awarded over 9,000 prizes and this year will be huge. We will be awarding thousands of prizes, including incredibly ‘suite’ grand prizes, making Suite-Stakes the most unique promotion in the industry,” said Phil Moore, senior vice president for Federated.

Eligible enrolled participants will have the chance to win one of thousands of prizes, such as an incredible ocean cruise, an ultimate St. Louis weekend featuring a Cardinals baseball game and the World of Outlaws race at Federated Auto Parts Raceway, a Get Dirty with Kenny driving experience and suite tickets to the 2024 Dirt Car Nationals in Florida.

“This year, there will also be sales team and member rewards and customer training prizes like a trip to Florida for the three-day hybrid/EV training workshop held at the new The Group Training Academy Training and Research Center,” said Moore. “We want to thank the Federated membership and participating vendor partners for their support of the Suite-Stakes promotion, and we can’t wait to give away thousands of ‘suite’ prizes to our loyal Federated customers.”

The Federated Suite-Stakes contest is open for enrollment and runs until June 30, 2023. To be eligible, participants must be a Federated customer in good standing and enrolled in the contest before the June 30 deadline. For more information, including the exciting prizes and sponsoring vendors, contact a Federated representative or visit [www.FederatedSuiteStakes.com](http://www.federatedsuitestakes.com/).
