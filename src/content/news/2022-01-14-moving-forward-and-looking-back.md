Moving into a new year always brings excitement. I am excited to work with Federated members and our manufacturer partners in 2022.

The past two years have certainly been challenging. We have survived many obstacles that have come our way and we may have actually come out better for the experience. We have found many ways to make our businesses stronger and more efficient and we have learned that uncertainty and fear can be overcome if we show courage and are willing to address them head-on. The tenacity shown by the automotive aftermarket and Federated members has truly been an inspiration.

I have had many great sources of inspiration in my life. There are things in my life that happen every day that I can thank my parents for. They taught me not only the basics of survival but so much more. They taught me how to be a good person, to be grateful for all things big and small, and to always know that I could accomplish anything if I was willing to work hard enough for it.

I will always be grateful to Art Fisher for allowing me to work at Federated. Art was a huge force in the automotive industry and a tremendous inspiration to me in my career at Federated. I have always marveled at Art’s ability to teach and instill confidence in his employees. Art was a forward thinker and very much ahead of his time in his vision of the aftermarket. I always associate Art with a quote by John Quincy Adams: “If your actions inspire others to dream more, learn more, do more, and become more, you are a leader.” Art was certainly a leader and January is always a painful reminder that Art has not been with us for 18 years now, but his teaching, his philosophies, and his wisdom have remained with us. It was a great privilege to work under his leadership.
