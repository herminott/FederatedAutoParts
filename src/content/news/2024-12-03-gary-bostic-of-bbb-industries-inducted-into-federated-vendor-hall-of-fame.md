Gary Bostic, director of group sales for BBB Industries, has been inducted into the Federated Auto Parts Vendor Hall of Fame. Bostic was presented with this distinguished recognition earlier this month in Las Vegas.

“Those who know him best describe Gary Bostic as a consummate professional that truly loves the industry and the friends he’s made. He’s a family man who often speaks of his grandkids. He’s honest, personable, hard-working, smart, dependable and trustworthy… in short, a quality human being,” said Bo Fisher, chairman of Federated Auto Parts. “The Federated Vendor Hall of Fame honors the best in our industry and Gary is truly among those outstanding leaders. His dedication, along with the support of the BBB team, has been crucial to our members’ success. We are grateful for their ongoing partnership with Federated and The Group.”

Since 2019, Bostic has served as a vice president of sales for BBB Industries and in 2022 moved to director of group sales for BBB Industries. Prior to the BBB Industries acquisition of Remy in 2019, Bostic held a variety of positions at Remy, including vice president of traditional sales and director of light vehicle aftermarket sales. He has more than 40 years of experience in the auto care industry in sales, ownership, distribution and the tire segment of the industry. Bostic has volunteered his time and expertise serving on several industry committees of various aftermarket organizations.

The Federated Vendor Hall of Fame recognizes one individual each year who demonstrates dedication to excellence, helping to make a difference for Federated Auto Parts and its membership.
