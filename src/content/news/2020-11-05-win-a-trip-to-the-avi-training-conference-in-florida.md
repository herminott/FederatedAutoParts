Federated Auto Parts wants to send one fortunate Federated Car Care Center to the 2021 AVI Training Conference. The winning shop in the Federated/AVI Learning Sweepstakes will choose an employee and a guest that will be treated to a three-day trip to Sanibel Harbour in Fort Myers, Florida from Jan. 14-17, 2021.

“The AVI Training Conference is the premier training event in the auto care industry, offering an assortment of seminars from some of the top instructors in the business,” said John Marcum, director of marketing for Federated. “We are excited to send one of our valued Federated Car Care Centers to Florida in January for the conference and some fun in the sun.”

To be eligible, Federated Car Care service professionals simply have to complete a course on The Group Training Academy website during the month of November. One entry will be earned for each completed course. The more classes are completed, the more chances are earned.

The AVI Training Conference includes two days of world-class training, opening and closing night receptions, and free time to spend on the beach. Conference tickets, lodging, meals, and airline arrangements for two are included as part of the three-day package. The seminar will be hosted at the beautiful Sanibel Harbour Marriott Resort & Spa and the AVI studios and will include a new technical track and a train-the-trainer day. Conference organizers are taking into account health safety guidelines. All seating for classes and meals will meet appropriate social distancing measures and masks will be required for all indoor activities.

To view available courses, visit [www.TheGroupTrainingAcademy.com](http://www.TheGroupTrainingAcademy.com).
