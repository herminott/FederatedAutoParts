Federated Auto Parts has kicked off the Federated 50/50 Challenge in conjunction with The Group Training Academy (TGTA). In addition to gaining knowledge from a series of informative “TechTalk Automotive Professionals 101” videos, those who enter the contest will be eligible to win a Bosch tool or a gift card reward just for watching.

The TechTalk Automotive Professionals 101 video series is sponsored by Bosch and presented by automotive expert John Gardner, host of the popular *Tech Garage* and *Motorhead Garage* television series on the MotorTrend Television Network. In the TechTalk video series, the four-to-six-minute videos focus on areas associated with ASE testing, from A1 engine repair through A8 engine performance. Every automotive system will be featured with explanations of how and why things work and why they sometimes do not work.

Participants who [register](http://www.federated5050challenge.com/) and watch the first 50 “TechTalk” videos in the Automotive 101 video series by Jan. 15, 2024, will be entered to win one of five Bosch cordless Bluetooth-compatible job-site radio and cordless impact wrenches as well as a chance to win one of 50, $50 gift cards. As an added bonus, the first 250 service professionals who complete the Federated 50/50 Challenge will receive a limited edition, TechTalk/Federated/Bosch logo cap in addition to chances to win the tool and gift card prizes.

“The new video series from The Group Training Academy is an incredibly informative tool to help service providers solve an assortment of service and repair issues and prepare for the various ASE certification tests,” said Phil Moore, senior vice president for Federated. “The Federated 50/50 Challenge is a fun way to encourage service professionals to watch these quick, educational videos, and we want to thank our partners at Bosch for providing us with incredible tools as prizes. Be sure to watch these first 50 videos and stay tuned for the next 50 in the series and even more great prizes.”

To enter the Federated 50/50 Challenge, visit [www.Federated5050Challenge.com](http://www.Federated5050Challenge.com). Once registered, go to the TGTA [website](https://thegrouptrainingacademy.com/ase_tip/?key=CqXoDrl6A2AEAFvYX8Qw454iBiDL6o&email=any.valid@email.com) to set up an account and start watching the Automotive 101 training videos. Those who already have a TGTA account must first [enter](https://www.federated5050challenge.com/) to be eligible to win.

For more information about the Federated 50/50 Challenge, and to enter for a chance to win, visit [www.federated5050challenge.com/](http://www.federated5050challenge.com/).
