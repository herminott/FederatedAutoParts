I am very excited to announce that behind-the-scenes work has begun for the 2022 Group National Meeting, which will be held “in-person” at the Gaylord Texan Hotel in Grapevine, Texas, April 24-28.

This will be the first “in-person” meeting since 2019 and although virtual meetings have presented us with a lifeline during the past two years, we have also realized some of the shortcomings and we are more than eager to get back to meeting with members and our supplier partners face-to-face.

The meeting will officially open on Monday, April 25 at 6:00 p.m. and will feature Vincent Poscente as the keynote speaker. Mr. Poscente is an expert on resiliency, which is an extremely timely topic. This year’s meeting will again feature full membership sessions, one-on-one meetings, a vendor expo (booth show), and an informative industry update. The meeting will conclude with the Toys for Tots Charity Golf Outing on Thursday, April 28th. You will also be provided with many opportunities to socialize and network throughout the course of the meeting.

Information regarding the meeting will be emailed to you very soon and will include instructions on how to register for the meeting and also how to make your room reservations. Information on how to choose your one-on-ones will follow a little later.

Studies have shown that face-to-face meetings have higher engagement, enforce existing social bonds, strengthen relationships, and build trust and understanding.

I look forward to seeing everyone in April!
