This past weekend I was invited to an Octoberfest celebration at a friend’s house. When I arrived at the event, parked at the front of the driveway was a Cybertruck. Admittedly, this is the first one I had actually seen other than in a magazine or in a YouTube video.

The truck was a brilliant lime green, which I later learned was a wrap that was purchased by the owner to protect the stainless-steel finish. The owner of the vehicle told me that prior to the truck being wrapped, he would spend in excess of a $100 dollars for cleaning products and then spend many hours working to remove fingerprints from the stainless-steel finish.

The owner then gave a demonstration which he referred to as “the light show.” Lively music started playing and the lights on the truck started flashing to a hip-hop beat. The light show lasted about three minutes and concluded with the “frunk” (front trunk) opening and closing. It was very entertaining.

I asked about the vehicle’s overall performance and the owner said he was very pleased so far, and of course, it was apparent that his children absolutely loved the futuristic, aerodynamic look. As I looked at this vehicle, the words that kept coming to mind were “unconventional” and “innovative.”

Then our friends jokingly asked, “So what parts will you be able to sell for this vehicle?”

When the owner of this vehicle needs to have a repair performed, this is the stern message that they will get.

*“The manufacturer of this vehicle does not allow the use of any used, recycled, alternative, aftermarket or third-party replacement parts.* *Use only new parts ordered directly from the manufacturer of the vehicle. The use of used, recycled, alternative, aftermarket or third-party replacement parts might compromise vehicle integrity and occupant safety.”*

Being conventional by nature, I have no desire to own a vehicle like the Cybertruck.  However, it certainly was fascinating to see one firsthand, but at the same time, it is disturbing to know t the owner of the vehicle does not have a choice when it comes time to repair their vehicle. How many car owners today are aware of this when they purchase a vehicle? That is why “Right to Repair” is so important. Consumers deserve to have a choice when it comes to repair. Consumers also deserve to have the right to dictate how the information provided by the vehicle they own is used.  Please continue to contact your legislators and urge them to put vehicle owners’ interests first by supporting Right to Repair legislation for the automotive repair industry.
