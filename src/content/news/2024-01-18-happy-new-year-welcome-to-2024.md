It is always exciting to begin a new year, but it can also be very hectic, which makes it the perfect time to pause, reflect and refocus.

As I reflect on 2023, there is no doubt that it was a big year for Federated.

January 1, 2023 was the official date of the joint venture with our partners at the Aftermarket Auto Parts Alliance and the formation of the Federated Alliance. Both the Federated staff and the Alliance staff spent a tremendous amount of time in 2023 working to identify the areas that we could both participate in and areas that would help both groups (and our channel partners) to realize significant cost savings. A good example was the Group National Meeting held in National Harbor, Maryland. Together with our partners from the Alliance and the Pronto Network, we feel this was one of our best and most successful meetings to date.

2023 was also a great year for our marketing team. They put together another very successful “Suite Stakes” promotion. This was year three for the Suite Stakes program, and with over 12,000 service centers participating, the program continues to grow and get better each year with more participants and more exciting prizes. The Federated marketing team is already busy working on the 2024 Suite Stakes promotion, which is scheduled to kick off in August.

2024 is sure to bring changes and new challenges, and it is reassuring to have a staff at Federated who can meet those challenges. We have such a great group of talented people who are dedicated to Federated and our members. At the beginning of each new year, there are a few questions that I always like to consider when setting my own goals.

- What lessons did I learn last year?
- What areas did I do well in and what areas need more work?
- What do I need to do to improve?
- What are some of the things that I hope to accomplish in 2024?

Maybe these questions can help you to set your 2024 goals. I wish all of you a very healthy, happy and successful new year! “The present, even if it is arduous, can be lived and accepted if it leads toward a goal, if we can be sure of this goal, and if this goal is great enough to justify the journey.” — Pope Benedict XVI
