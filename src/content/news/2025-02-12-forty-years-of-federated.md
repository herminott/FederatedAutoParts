When I came to work for Federated in 1985, it would have been hard to imagine that I would be writing this article 40 years later. Where does the time go?

The past 40 years have been incredible, but Federated’s 40th anniversary goes beyond marking time. It serves as a testament to the dedication, hard work and unwavering support from everyone who has made this journey possible.  
Beginning with Art Fisher, our founder, who had the foresight to start a program distribution group established on the principal of line commonality and vendor partnership relationships, to our Federated members, who are committed to growing their business and serving their customers’ needs.

Over the years, Federated’s leadership has changed but our founding principals have remained the same. Our dedicated staff has always been committed to helping our members grow and remain competitive. We have become stronger through our association with the Pronto Network in the Automotive Parts Services Group and our joint venture with the Aftermarket Auto Parts Alliance.

From ambitious beginnings to overcoming challenges that tested our resilience, the unwavering support and dedication of the Federated membership has allowed us to remain a strong force in the automotive aftermarket.

We have come a long way together. Thank you for your trust and your support!
