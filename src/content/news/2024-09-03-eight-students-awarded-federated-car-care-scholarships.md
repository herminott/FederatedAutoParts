Federated Car Care scholarships have been awarded to eight exceptional students for the 2024-25 academic year.

- Jacob Arnold (Robstown, Texas)
- Ryan Baldwin (Rupert, West Virginia)
- Kylie Caverly (Howell, Michigan)
- William Chambers (Wake Forest, North Carolina)
- Gabriela DeJesus (Newtown, Connecticut)
- Sophia Manfredi (New Freedom, Pennsylvania)
- Michael Phelps (Cincinnati, Ohio)
- Matthew Tanner (Buena Vista, Virginia)

Awarded to the employees or children of Federated Car Care Center members, the Federated Car Care scholarships are funded by Fisher Auto Parts and administered by the University of the Aftermarket Foundation in memory of the late Art Fisher, founder of Federated Auto Parts.

To be considered, applicants had to meet the criteria outlined in the [Federated](http://www.automotivescholarships.com/scholarshiptype.cfm?type=45) section of the Automotive Aftermarket Scholarship Central website and name Federated in the sponsor section of their completed application. As a bonus, each application was also considered for all scholarships listed on [AutomotiveScholarships.com](http://www.automotivescholarships.com/) where the qualifications were met.

“Throughout our history, Federated has been dedicated to providing educational opportunities,” said Sue Godschalk, president of Federated Auto Parts. “It is with great pleasure that we present the Federated Car Care scholarships to this remarkable group of young individuals. In addition to their extraordinary academic accomplishments, these students have also demonstrated exceptional leadership in their communities. We extend our heartfelt congratulations to them and offer our best wishes as they embark on the upcoming school year.”Top of Form
