I recently attended my high school reunion and I was amazed at how easy it was to reconnect with my former classmates. In no time, we were all laughing, talking, and telling stories from our high school years. It was very evident that we had all missed seeing each other and it was a lot of fun to catch-up and learn what everyone had been doing over the past few years.

Seeing my classmates reminded me that returning to “in-person” meetings at AWDA this year will have much the same feeling. It will be good to have the opportunity to reconnect with our manufacturer partners, business associates, and friends throughout the industry.

While technology has helped us stay connected, there is nothing comparable to face-to-face contact while conducting goal-specific meetings. I am looking forward to gaining new insights into today’s business environment and learning how others are handling the challenges that we are currently facing in trying to supply our customers with products.

I am also looking forward to the awards ceremony and the timely presentations from the most respected names in the industry. It is going to be much like old home week and I am looking forward to a great reunion.
