Federated Auto Parts is expanding technician training opportunities for member stores and their shop customers.

## What's new

- Regional hands-on brake and undercar clinics
- Short digital modules for counter staff
- Updated catalog tips for late-model coverage

Training remains a core part of how Federated helps independent distributors stay competitive. Ask your local Federated representative for the next clinic in your market.
