I have a nice feature on my cell phone that displays a picture each day of a place that I have traveled, and each picture shows the date that it was taken. It is a daily reminder of how my life has been transformed since the pandemic. When I see a vineyard in Italy, or the vast landscape in Iceland, or the verdant, green meadows of Ireland, or the magnificent buildings in St. Petersburg, it is a rude awakening, and it is hard to comprehend the limitations on the freedom we once had to travel safely to these beautiful and remarkable places. While this is a nice feature, it is always a stark reminder that I have no pictures like this for 2020. The pandemic has caused my world to shrink.

It is my hope that when all of this passes, we can eventually forget the negatives and dwell only on the positives. Although there is no replacement for personal connections, we should certainly appreciate and remember how technology has allowed us to remain connected to our jobs, our friends, and our loved ones. Then, on the other hand, wouldn’t it be nice to see someone in a restaurant without their phone in front of them? Wouldn’t it be nice to see kids playing outside instead of inside playing video games?

I hope everyone remembers the risks that the doctors, nurses, and first responders have taken and that we always appreciate the demand that was placed on all those in the health care profession. I hope more people remember to say ‘thank you’ to those who keep us well. I hope we remember the acts of kindness we have heard of and have witnessed during this time and that we continue to carry those acts with us.

I hope we remember the fear that the pandemic has caused and that those reluctant to vaccinate see the value in modern medicine and understand the consequences that can happen when we do not have herd immunity to protect the most vulnerable. I hope we remember that we need to seek out valid and reliable sources of information and not believe that everything we see on the news or read on social media is true.

I hope we remember the resilience of the human spirit and appreciate those who have helped us see light in dark times. May we always remember the things in life that are truly important and may we always remember to stay hopeful.
