To help spread some holiday cheer to its Facebook followers, Federated Auto Parts is once again conducting its Facebook diecast contest. From now through Dec. 15, Federated will be giving away limited-edition Federated 1963 Volkswagen van diecast collectibles.

“The annual Federated Holiday Diecast promotion is an opportunity to thank our loyal Facebook followers, while at the same time, helping the lucky winners celebrate the holidays with the gift of a special Federated Diecast,” said John Marcum, director of marketing of Federated Auto Parts. “This year we are starting the contest early so more lucky winners will receive one of our new diecast vehicles, the perfect gift for a collector or a great stocking stuffer.” The Federated holiday diecast contest is underway and five winners will be announced each Friday on the Federated Facebook pages for six weeks with the final winners announced on Dec. 15. To enter, visit the [Federated Auto Parts](https://www.facebook.com/pages/Federated-Auto-Parts/104297261466?ref=hl) and [Federated Car Care](https://www.facebook.com/FederatedCarCare) on Facebook and click on the mobile link in the first post to fill in the entry form. Extra entries can be earned by sharing the contest with Facebook friends.
