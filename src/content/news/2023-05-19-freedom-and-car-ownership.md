I just recently returned to the office from vacation. After having to postpone a previously planned trip to Portugal and Spain in 2020 due to COVID-19, I was finally able to check those two countries off my bucket list.

Both Portugal and Spain are very beautiful. Acres and acres of grapevines and olive trees cover the hills and valleys. If you live in the countryside, chances are there will be an orange or lemon tree in your backyard. If you live in the city, there might be a smaller lemon or orange tree on your balcony along with other beautiful plants and herbs. There are beautiful cathedrals, monasteries, and parks.

However, if you do live in the city, what you will not typically have is an automobile.  There is no room for parking, parking lots, or parking garages. People who live in the city walk, bike, or take the train everywhere they need to go.

You do see a few of the normal taxis (or lifts as they are called there) and of course tour buses, but many people who live in the city never own or drive an automobile.

After being involved in the automotive industry for most of my adult life and a driver since the age of 16, I find it fascinating that there are people who can function daily without the aid of an automobile.

For me, my vehicle gives me a sense of personal freedom and choice. I can go anywhere I choose at any time. I get behind the wheel and I am empowered. I am in control of my destiny. Therefore, it is important to me that everything on my vehicle works. Regular maintenance is a must. I am a repair shop’s dream customer.  So, whether the vehicle you drive is a Chevy or a Ford, a car, a truck, or a sport utility vehicle, or whether it is a hybrid, electric, or a combustible engine vehicle, the automobile is and will always be an important part of our lives. How fortunate for our industry and how fortunate we are to have this freedom.
