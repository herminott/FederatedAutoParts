NEXUS Automotive International presented Federated Auto Parts with its Milestone Achievement Award in recognition of Federated’s 40th anniversary. The award was presented during the recent NEXUS Business Forum in Abu Dhabi.

“We greatly appreciate NEXUS Automotive International for recognizing Federated’s 40th anniversary milestone with this esteemed award,” said Sue Godschalk, CEO of Federated Auto Parts. “By working in partnership with NEXUS, the Automotive Parts Services Group, including Federated, is able to learn from a global community of aftermarket suppliers, distributions and innovation providers, and share practical, sustainable solutions with members to help them in their local markets.”

The NEXUS Business Forum was held April 8-10. The gathering brought together over 700 aftermarket professionals, including more than 85 suppliers and some 215 member companies. Jeff Koviak, CEO of the Automotive Parts Services Group, accepted the award on behalf of Federated.
