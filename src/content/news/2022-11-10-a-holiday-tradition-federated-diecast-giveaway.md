With the holiday season upon us, Federated is conducting its annual Facebook diecast contest. Now through Dec. 14, Federated will be giving away an eclectic group of limited-edition diecasts, including racing and classic cars.

“We are excited to bring back our holiday diecast contest as a special way to thank our loyal Facebook followers,” said John Marcum, director of marketing of Federated Auto Parts. “These diecasts are a perfect gift for a collector or make a great stocking stuffer. What makes this year’s contest even more fun is that each winner will be surprised by the diecast they receive.”

The Federated holiday diecast begins today. Four winners will be announced each Wednesday on both of the Federated Facebook pages, two winners from each page, for five weeks with the final winners announced on Dec. 14. To enter, visit [Federated Auto Parts](https://www.facebook.com/pages/Federated-Auto-Parts/104297261466?ref=hl) and [Federated Car Care](https://www.facebook.com/FederatedCarCare) on Facebook and click on the mobile link in the first post to fill in the entry form. Extra entries can be earned by sharing the contest with Facebook friends.
