Federated is celebrating the holidays by giving its Facebook followers the chance to win a limited-edition 1963 Corvette Stingray in the Federated “12 Days of Christmas” Facebook contest.

“We are excited to continue our holiday tradition of giving away Federated diecasts to our loyal Facebook followers,” said Phil Moore, senior vice president of Federated Auto Parts. “This one-of-kind collectible makes a great stocking stuffer for a loved one or a great present for yourself. We thank our Facebook friends for their continued support of Federated Auto Parts and wish them good luck in this fun contest and the happiest of holidays.”

The Federated “12 Days of Christmas” contest runs until December 15, 2020. Two winners will be announced each day from December 4 through December 15 on each of the [Federated Auto Parts](https://www.facebook.com/FederatedAP/) or [Federated Car Care](https://www.facebook.com/FederatedCarCare) Facebook pages. To enter, click on the mobile [link](https://woobox.com/d9ix2n) and fill in the entry form. Extra entries can be earned by sharing the contest with Facebook friends.
