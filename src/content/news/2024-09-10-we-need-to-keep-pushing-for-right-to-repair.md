Labor Day is an annual celebration which occurs on the first Monday in September. Contrary to what the name suggests, most of us do not work on Labor Day. The day has been set aside as a federal holiday in observance of the achievements of American workers and to recognize the contributions that workers have made to America’s strength and prosperity.

Labor Day also brings to mind the importance of Right to Repair. Right to Repair not only affects the workforce that is associated with the automotive industry, but it also affects the workers who depend on their automobile to get them to work each day. If repair shops are not given access to vehicle data, car owners will be left with no choice other than to have their vehicle repaired by the dealer. The repairs will ultimately cost more, and the wait time will be longer.

Federal legislation is desperately needed to secure the rights of every vehicle owner and protect the livelihood of 273,000 shops and 900,000 technicians that service the 293 million vehicles on the road.

At the upcoming The Group National Meeting, which is being held November 2-6 in conjunction with AWDA and AAPEX, there will be a tremendous focus on Right to Repair. Please continue to scan the Right to Repair QR Code and send a message to your legislator and encourage everyone that you know to do the same. We need your continued support to change Right to Repair from a cause into a law.
