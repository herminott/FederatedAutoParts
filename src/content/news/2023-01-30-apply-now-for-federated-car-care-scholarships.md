Applications for the Federated Car Care Scholarship program for the 2023-24 school year are now being accepted at [AutomotiveScholarships.com](https://automotivescholarships.com/).

The [scholarships](https://automotivescholarships.com/scholarships/federated-car-care-scholarship) are funded by Fisher Auto Parts in memory of Art Fisher, founder of Federated Auto Parts and the Federated Car Care program, and the deadline to apply is March 31, 2023. Qualified candidates should specify “Federated” on their application when applying online.

Administered by the University of the Aftermarket Foundation ([UAF](http://www.UofA-Foundation.org)), the scholarships will be awarded to employees and children of active Federated Car Care Center members attending a two- or four-year accredited college, an ASE/NATEF certified post-secondary automotive, heavy-duty, or collision technician training program or any licensed and accredited vocational school. Students graduating from high school in 2023 and heading to any of these post-secondary programs are also eligible.

“We are honored to once again offer these scholarship opportunities to children and employees of Car Care members to help them advance their education,” said Bo Fisher, chairman, and CEO of Fisher Auto Parts. “We want to thank the University of the Aftermarket Foundation for helping to facilitate the application process, and we encourage all eligible candidates to submit their applications as soon as possible. Federated Car Care scholarships have gone to very worthy recipients in the past and I’m sure this year will be no different.”

Federated is one of more than 30 other organizations awarding scholarships on the UAF Automotive Aftermarket Scholarship Central [website](http://www.automotivescholarships.com). As an added benefit for students who apply at this site, each completed application will be considered by every scholarship where the candidate meets the qualifications.
