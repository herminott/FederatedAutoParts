After a one-year hiatus due to the pandemic, we were so pleased to hear that Leadership 2.0 would be back for 2021. We are also pleased to announce that Federated will have three scholarship recipients this year.

Congratulations to Arnoldo Ventura, president of Frontera Radiators and Parts in El Paso, Texas. Arnoldo has displayed an excellent business acumen for running his company which has locations in both the U.S. and Mexico. Arnoldo is excited to have the opportunity to attend Leadership 2.0 and he is looking forward to meeting other scholarship recipients from the industry.

Javier Alcantar is the second scholarship recipient for Leadership 2.0. Javier is a regional shop manager for Frontera. Javier is a young professional with a true passion for the automotive industry. Javier has worked as an automotive technician and in counter sales. Through Leadership 2.0, Javier is looking forward to learning how to help his fellow shop customers grow their businesses.

The third scholarship recipient is Shao Dyi. Shao is the general manager for Parts Warehouse Distributors, Inc. in San Francisco, California. In his leadership position at Parts Warehouse Distributors, Shao has accrued a wealth of knowledge and experience and he is constantly looking to enhance that knowledge. Shao is very much looking forward to being in a classroom setting and having the opportunity to learn from other industry professionals.

All three of the scholarship recipients are excellent choices and we are very grateful to the University of the Aftermarket Foundation for offering these scholarships to our Federated WD’s and service dealers.
