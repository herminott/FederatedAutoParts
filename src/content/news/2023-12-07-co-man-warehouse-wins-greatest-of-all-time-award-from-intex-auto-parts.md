It was Boris Pasternak, a Russian writer and poet who said, “Surprise is the greatest gift which life can grant us.”

Surprise was the word of the day when the Federated Co-Man Warehouse staff received the “Greatest of All Time” award from Intex Auto Parts, a Federated member located in San Jose, California.

Intex Auto Parts has been a long-time supporter of the Federated Co-Man Warehouse and Kenny Ho, operations manager for Intex, could not say enough good things about the staff and the co-man warehouse.

“We are happy to be a part of Federated and being able to order from the co-man warehouse with consistently high fill, high accuracy and the least headache,” said Ho. “No other vendor or warehouse comes close. I shudder to think if we didn’t have the co-man warehouse during Covid how things would have been. It’s made all the difference.” Co-Man General Manager Julie Wells and her staff constantly strive to offer the best service possible to all Federated member participants such as Intex Auto Parts. The award came as a complete surprise to the co-man team and the recognition as “Vendor of the Decade” was very much appreciated and well deserved.
