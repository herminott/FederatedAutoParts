**How did you begin your career in the aftermarket?**

I started in the automotive aftermarket industry in 1990 as a temporary employee in the accounting department of a privately owned wholesale distributor.  As soon as my contract ended, the company hired me, and I’ve stayed in this industry since then. I joined the Federated team in 2014.

**What is the proudest accomplishment of your career, thus far?**

I’ve had some great opportunities to learn many functions in this industry from store operations, warehouse controls, financial analysis, vendor programs, vendor rebates and many other tasks. One thing all of this has taught me is the importance of reliable, timely data that provides important information for both our internal and external business partners. One of my proudest accomplishments is the creation of an internal database that houses several years’ worth of financial information that can be provided quickly to both management and our members.

**What is your best advice to other women who want to excel in the automotive aftermarket?**

This is a great industry for woman who are interested in a challenging job with multiple opportunities.  Learn as much as you can about all functions of the business.  Find mentors to help you along the way. Discover your talents and use them to improve your position, your department and your company.  Ask to take on more, learn more and do more. Be bold!

**What are some of the ways you have seen the industry support women in the aftermarket?**

I’ve seen many changes since I started in this industry. Women have stepped up and taken on many roles that were primarily male positions.  We have women working the counter selling parts, working in sales, even presidents of companies, like our own at Federated.  It makes me smile to walk into an auto parts store and have a woman help me find the part I need. Everything is so technical, and it impresses me every time.

**What is one thing the industry could do better or do differently to support women in the aftermarket?**

Women who are currently in our industry can make a huge impact here. Mentor your female employees; teach them everything you know. Promote them into key roles. As we saw in the pandemic years, this industry is key to the success of our communities and even our country. Women in the workforce need stable jobs to support their families. The automotive aftermarket industry is great place to make that happen.
