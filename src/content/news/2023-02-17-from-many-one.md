We are very excited that our 2023 The Group National Conference and Expo is only a few weeks away. This is The Group’s seventh annual conference and this year’s meeting will be held March 27-30 at the Gaylord Resort and Conference Center in National Harbor, Maryland.

One of the challenges we are presented with each year is choosing an appropriate meeting theme. Since we were going to be very close to the U.S. capitol, we thought it might be fitting to choose a theme that represents patriotism and The Automotive Parts Services Group. Thanks to J.R. Bishop, a member of the Federated marketing team, for presenting the possibility of using E Pluribus Unum. As it turned out, we all agreed that this Latin phrase is not only rich in the history of our country, but it also provides a fitting description of the Automotive Parts Services Group (The Group) of which now consists of the Pronto Network and the Federated Alliance.

E Pluribus Unum describes an action: Many uniting into one, but it is sometimes more loosely translated as “one from many.” It was once the national motto of the United States of America and referenced the fact that a cohesive single nation was formed as the result of the original 13 colonies joining together, one of which happens to be the state of Maryland where our meeting will be held. It may also just be a coincidence that E Pluribus Unum has 13 letters. The national motto was changed by an act of the United States Congress in 1956; however,

E Pluribus Unum does still appear on U.S. coins.

Much like the actions of the 13 original colonies, the Pronto Network and the Federated Alliance have become associated under the Automotive Parts Services Group for the betterment of both organizations. We will be able to partner together in the areas where it makes the most sense in order to provide cost savings to our members. Working together and becoming “one from many” will help us to remain strong and competitive in today’s marketplace. Attendees can count on this year’s conference to be the best one yet, so if you have not already registered for the meeting and made your hotel reservations, be sure and do so before the March 1 deadline. I look forward to seeing everyone at The Group National Conference in March.
