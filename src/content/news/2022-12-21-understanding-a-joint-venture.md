Until recently, I had never given a lot of thought to the meaning of a “joint venture.”

The definition of a joint venture is “a business arrangement in which two or more parties agree to pool their resources for the purpose of accomplishing a specific task.”

What are some of the reasons for forming a joint venture?

- To leverage resources
- To reduce costs
- To combine expertise

Federated Auto Parts Distributors has officially announced that a joint venture has been formed with the Aftermarket Auto Parts Alliance. Both Federated and the Alliance have many talented employees and there are so many areas that we can work together on to save costs while both groups still maintain their individual identity. Having access to new knowledge and expertise along with access to greater resources will help both Federated and the Alliance to continue to achieve our common goal. That goal is to serve our customers at a higher level and continue to offer the best products and services so that we can remain competitive in today’s marketplace. I am very excited to begin working on projects with the Alliance and I think 2023 will be a dynamic year.
