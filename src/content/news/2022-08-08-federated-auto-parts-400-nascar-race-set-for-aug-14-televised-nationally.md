**WHAT**

NASCAR Federated Auto Parts 400

**WHEN**

Sunday, Aug. 14, 3:00 p.m. ET

**WHERE**

Richmond Raceway – Richmond, Virginia

**TELEVISION COVERAGE**

Live on USA Network and Peacocktv.com

The annual NASCAR Federated Auto Parts 400, scheduled for Sunday, Aug. 14 at 3:00 p.m. ET., will be televised nationally by the USA Network and streamed by Peacocktv.com. The race marks Federated’s 27th consecutive year of involvement in NASCAR.
