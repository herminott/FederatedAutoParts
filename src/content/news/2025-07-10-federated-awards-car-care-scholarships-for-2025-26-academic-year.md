Federated Auto Parts has announced the recipients of its Federated Car Care scholarships for the 2025-26 academic year. Awarded to the employees or children of Federated Car Care Center members, the scholarships are funded by Fisher Auto Parts, administered by the University of the Aftermarket Foundation and awarded in memory of the late Art Fisher, founder of Federated Auto Parts.

“Since Federated was founded 40 years ago, we have been committed to supporting students with educational opportunities,” said Sue Godschalk, CEO of Federated Auto Parts. “We congratulate these five outstanding students who are exceptional students and have proven to be actively involved at their respective schools and in their communities.”

The following are the Federated Car Care scholarship honorees for 2025-26:

- Ryan Baldwin of Fairmont, West Virginia
- Rylee Coffman of Jefferson City, Tennessee
- Natalie Manfredi of York, Pennsylvania
- Brooke Perry of Colchester, Vermont
- Michael Phelps of Cincinnati, Ohio

To be considered for a Federated Car Care scholarship, applicants had to meet the criteria outlined in the [Federated](http://www.automotivescholarships.com/scholarshiptype.cfm?type=45) section of the Automotive Aftermarket Scholarship Central website and name Federated in the sponsor section of their completed application. As a bonus, each application was also considered for all scholarships listed on [AutomotiveScholarships.com](http://www.automotivescholarships.com/) where the qualifications were met.

To learn more about Federated Car Care Scholarships or other scholarships being awarded by the University of the Aftermarket Foundation, visit [AutomotiveScholarships.com](http://www.automotivescholarships.com/).
