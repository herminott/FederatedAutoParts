It is certainly an understatement to say that 2020 has been an unusual year. As we enter the holiday season, I hope you will remember that for many years Federated has been participating in and supporting the Toys for Tots program. Created in 1947, the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve Toys for Tots program distributes millions of toys each year to less fortunate children. Your donation is especially important this year when so many families are suffering financial hardship due to COVID-19.

For the “hard to buy for” people on your Christmas list, a donation to Toys for Tots is the perfect gift. You can make a donation *“In Honor”* of those people. If you want to pay tribute to a loved one at Christmas, they also have an *“In Memory”* donation.

Please consider helping the U.S. Marines to bring the joy of Christmas and send a message of hope to America’s less fortunate children. Did you know that 97 percent of your contribution goes to providing toys, books, and other gifts to less fortunate children? The remaining three percent is used to cover fundraising expenses. Not one donated dollar goes to pay for salaries or any other manpower costs.

It is easy to make your contribution online by going to the following website: [ToysforTots.org/Donate](https://www.toysfortots.org/donate/). This website allows you to contribute monetarily or you can also shop, select, and donate the perfect toy with the new Virtual Toy Box.

Every child deserves a little Christmas!

*“For it is in giving that we receive.”  ~ St. Francis of Assisi*
