After what seems like years of Zoom meetings, things are finally looking up for business travel. Studies had estimated that business travel would still be down as much as 50 percent at the start of 2021. However, after a year away from other people, we are all craving that face-to-face interaction, and quite possibly, business trips could be back on the horizon. This optimism is due largely in part to the vaccine rollout and the decline in COVID-19 cases. This is definitely good news for those of us who are Covid fatigued.

We received more good news in the recent announcement that the 2021 AWDA Conference is being planned as an “in person” event in conjunction with AAPEX at the Venetian Resort & Casino in Las Vegas, Nevada Oct. 30-Nov. 4.

I have saved the best news for last. If you are not a member of the Auto Care Association, this is your chance to become a member “free of charge.” Federated is covering the Auto Care Association membership fees for all Federated members, both current and new.

If you are interested in becoming a member of the Auto Care Association at no cost, it is very simple to join. Your company will need to set up a profile in the Auto Care Association system.

You can create your [my.autocare.org](https://my.autocare.org/account/login.aspx) profile in less than two minutes; [creating an account](https://my.autocare.org/account/login.aspx) will walk you through the steps. Once you have created your profile, we will be able to move you over to the Federated Group, and your membership fee is automatically paid.

I hope those of you who are not currently a member of the Auto Care Association will take advantage of this offer.

If you have any questions or need assistance in setting up your profile in the Auto Care Association system, please give me a call.
