Nearly 200 Federated Suite-Stakes winners, members, vendors and guests recently set sail on an Alaskan adventure. Longtime Federated spokesperson and NASCAR legend Kenny Schrader was on board the Norwegian Bliss to greet and celebrate with the contest winners.

“The Federated Alaskan Expedition Cruise was an incredible experience made extra special with Kenny and his wife Ann along for the ride,” said J.R. Bishop, director of event marketing and member services for Federated. “The freestyle nature of the cruise let our guests relax, enjoy the ports and live it up at their own pace, while the Federated group activities, like an exclusive ‘Deal or No Deal’ event, gave us a chance to enjoy time together.”

The Alaskan voyage featured memorable excursions to such scenic locations as Juneau, Ketchikan and Sitka as well as numerous on-board activities, including unique dining options, entertaining performances and special events held exclusively for Federated guests. Highlights of the trip included wildlife treks, whale watching and sailing to the picturesque Dawes Glacier at the head of Endicott Arm Fjord.

“Since we started the Federated Suite-Stakes program, we have awarded over 17,000 prizes, and the Alaskan Expedition Cruise is one of the best yet. We thank Federated customers, members and vendor partners for making the Suite-Stakes program an ongoing success,” said Bishop. “The 2025 Suite-Stakes program begins August 1 and will feature exciting trips and prizes. We encourage all interested shops to contact a Federated representative to get in on the action.”
