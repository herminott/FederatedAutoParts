Your life can change in an instant. A true testament to that statement is the devastating effect that Hurricane Ian had on the people living in southwest Florida. With winds ranging from 112 -140 miles per hour, hurricane Ian dumped 10 or more inches of rain across 3,500 square miles on the day of landfall. Fort Myers experienced 7.21 feet of storm surge, breaking the previous record of 3.36 feet during Hurricane Gabrielle in 2001.

Many people who had moved to the Fort. Myers and neighboring Cape Coral areas had left permanent homes in other parts of the country and moved to those areas to live out their golden years. Although thankful just to be alive and safe, they are now faced with a devastating loss. While many of us sat in the comfort of our homes, thousands of people were uncertain about their own fate and the fate of their loved ones.

There is some comfort in knowing that there are many organizations that can help Florida residents during this time of recovery. FEMA, the Salvation Army, Habitat for Humanity, and many others too numerous to mention. But one worth mentioning is our own Automotive Aftermarket Charitable Foundation (AACF) who are raising funds on behalf of victims of Hurricane Ian.

The AACF was founded in 1959 to assist automotive aftermarket members and their families who suffer life-changing events and encounter tough times. The AACF can help people in our industry who desperately need help, but the AACF depends on companies and individuals throughout our industry for our support and contributions.

We are so thankful that our Federated member, Ft. Myers Automotive, and The Group Training Academy (AVI) were both safe and sustained little to no damage.

Someone’s life can change in an instant and we all need to work together to ensure that if it does, AACF can provide the assistance needed.  Please go to the AACF’s website and make your donation today (<https://www.aftermarketcharity.org/donate>).
