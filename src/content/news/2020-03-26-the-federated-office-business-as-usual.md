Every day for weeks now we have all been looking forward to updates on the Coronavirus with the hope of hearing some good news. A sprinkle of hope came to me recently when I first heard that they were ready to begin Phase 1 in a clinical trial to test a vaccine that is designed to protect against the coronavirus disease. Maybe just a tiny spec of light at the end of what might be a very long and dark tunnel.

During this trying and difficult time, it goes without saying, that we are all concerned about the health and wellbeing of our staff. We are fortunate that our individual workspaces at the Federated office are ample enough to allow us to work within our own space without intruding on our neighboring co-workers. We are following all of the recommended precautions; hand washing, wiping down our work surfaces and phones intermittently throughout the day, and of course, hand sanitizer abounds on everyone’s desk and in our common areas.

The Federated staff is committed to business as usual and we want to assure Federated members that we will continue with our day-to-day operations. As always, our dedicated team of professionals are here to provide any support that Federated members might need.

On behalf of the Federated staff, our hearts go out to all those who have suffered the loss of a family member, a loved one, or an acquaintance due to this terrible virus. We continue to keep everyone in our thoughts and prayers. Stay well!
