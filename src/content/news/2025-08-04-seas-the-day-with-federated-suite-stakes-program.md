The 2025 Federated Suite-Stakes promotion has begun, and lots of unbelievable prizes will be awarded, including ultimate grand prize trips to Cabo San Lucas and the Federated DIRTCar Nationals.

“Since we first started the Federated Suite-Stakes program, we have awarded a whopping 17,000 prizes,” said Phil Moore, senior vice president for Federated. “This year’s ‘Seas the Day’ Suite-Stakes program promises to be the best one yet with even more chances to win one of nearly 2,000 incredible prizes.”

In addition to all-inclusive trips to Cabo and VIP DIRTCar Nationals experiences, other exciting prizes include Federated diecast collectibles, World of Outlaws shirts, hats and gloves as well as gift cards, special swag and lots more.

“We want to thank the Federated members and vendor partners for their support in making the annual Suite-Stakes program such a success,” continued Moore. “We encourage all Federated customers to sign up now, so they don’t miss out on one of the best and most fun promotions in the industry.”

To be eligible, participants must be a Federated customer in good standing and enrolled in the sweepstakes by Oct. 31, 2025. For more details, contact a Federated representative or visit [www.FederatedSuiteStakes.com](http://www.federatedsuitestakes.com/).
