Congratulations to the Federated marketing team on conducting a very successful Federated Suite-Stakes Promotion, which ran from March 1st through June 30th.

Phil and his team members, John Marcum, JR Bishop, and Bob Resco, along with Al Joyner who provided data support, all spent a tremendous amount of time and talent in putting together the Federated Suite-Stakes, a sales-driven promotion designed to be easily used by all Federated members.

Over 11,000 Federated customers signed up to participate in the Federated Suite-Stakes, the biggest rewards program Federated Auto Parts has ever conducted for its customers.

Federated awarded more than 4,500 prizes, which included the “Super Suite” and “Suite” grand prizes.

The winners of the five “Super Suite” grand prizes will have an all-expense paid trip for two to the Federated Auto Parts 400 Salute to First Responders on September 11th. They will watch the NASCAR playoff race in the luxury of the Federated Suite at the Richmond Raceway.

The winners of the five “Suite” grand prizes also will have an all-expense paid trip for two to the World of Outlaws Ironman 55 with suite tickets and a bonus “Get Dirty with Kenny” dirt track driving experience.

Great job marketing team!
