What exactly is a Challenge Coin? They are usually round in shape and unique in that it bears an organization’s insignia or emblem and it represents the unity of an organization. They are exchanged or awarded in recognition of a task performed or a special achievement.

And so, we present the E Pluribus Unum Challenge Coin. You might be curious to know how one might go about obtaining one of these extremely rare mementos. It is really very simple.

- Visit the Toys for Tots booth at The Group National Conference and make a donation of $25 or more. You will receive one Challenge Coin.

- Visit the University of the Aftermarket booth at the meeting and make a donation of $25 to the Coffee Club or the AACF and you will receive one Challenge Coin for each organization you donate to.

- Scan the Right to Repair QR code at the Auto Care booth and send it to the congressman in your area and you will receive one Challenge Coin or make a $25 donation to ACPAC and you will receive another Challenge Coin.

- At the Smithsonian event, we will randomly award gift cards to anyone who can show us their Challenge Coin when asked.

- Ask someone to show you their Challenge Coin and if they do not have one, they have to buy you a drink (free drinks at receptions are not acceptable).

There are a few rules that need to be followed.

- You are not allowed to give someone else your Challenge Coin. It is your coin, your challenge.

- You are not allowed to drill holes in your Challenge Coin to wear as a bracelet, necklace, earring(s), or belt buckle.

- You must always carry your Challenge Coin. Never leave your Challenge Coin unattended. There are no exceptions to this rule.

Warning: If you are found guilty of one of these rule infractions, or you break Challenge Coin etiquette, there will be serious consequences to pay. Unless you have deep pockets, you do not want to be responsible for everyone’s bar tab.

Remember: It is not uncommon to show others your Challenge Coin to start up a conversation with someone who also has a Challenge Coin, and this could lead to building a new relationship or it might even present a business opportunity.

The Challenge Coin will also serve as a souvenir to remind you of the events that took place at the 2023 Group National Conference, which might include an act of kindness you performed by making a donation to a worthy cause, or a new business associate you met, a great keynote speaker, great one-on-one meetings or being the recipient of an absolutely free gift card.

I look forward to seeing you soon!
