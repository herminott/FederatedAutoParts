Our family recently celebrated my mother’s 90th birthday. During her party, and as we all reminisced, I could not help thinking how much the world has changed in her lifetime. Born during the Great Depression, my mother has lived through many major world events such as World War II, the Korean War, and the Vietnam War. During the early years of her life, she lived without a telephone, a television, and her parents never drove or owned a car. She has lived during a time when travel has progressed from primarily personal vehicles and coal-fired locomotives to an age of major highway systems and air travel. She has seen the news clip of the first man to walk on the moon, she has seen the Twin Towers fall and most recently, she experienced the lockdown and mask-up due to COVID-19 at the assisted living facility where she resides.

My mother was born in the age where most women worked in the home. I was a junior in high school when my mother got her first job at the age of 39 and I can still remember how excited she was.

While we have not seen the 90 years of change that my mother has, we have seen substantial change over the past several years. Women have taken a larger role in the automotive industry. When I see all of the very talented and dedicated women recently featured in aftermarketNews as 2021 Women at the Wheel, it makes me very proud to see these individuals recognized for their contribution to the automotive industry. They are all working hard and making a difference. The Federated staff and membership extend special congratulations to Ashlee Arnold, vice president of Arnold Oil Company in Austin, Texas for being chosen as one of the 2021 Women at the Wheel.

Congratulations to all of the 2021 Women at the Wheel!
