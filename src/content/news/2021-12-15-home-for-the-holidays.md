I find it hard to believe that it is already December. I love being home for the holidays. Shopping for that perfect gift, decorating, and baking are but a few of the wonderful traditions that I learned from my mother. I always count my blessings, but maybe a little more so during the holiday season. I also remember there are many who are not as fortunate as I am, and I pause and let my thoughts and prayers go out to those who have lost loved ones during the past year.

Coming out of 2020, I was uncertain what challenges might lie ahead. The residual effects from the pandemic certainly played a major role in 2021. Shortages due to supply chain disruption were further enhanced by container shortages and labor shortages.

But there were a lot of positives. We saw different variations of a return to some sense of normalcy. Businesses cautiously reopened and we are able to socialize again. More people began to feel comfortable with travel and we were actually able to attend an “in-person” meeting as we participated in AAPEX and AWDA. 2021 felt much like dipping your toe in to test the temperature of the water. You proceeded cautiously but with optimism and the hope that you were going to be safe.

As 2021 comes to a close, it is also a good time to remember those who are less fortunate and in need. A contribution to the Toys for Tots Charity would be greatly appreciated.

Perseverance, resilience, and faith in ourselves and in our community; that is what we have all experienced in the past year. These are the qualities given to us by our founding fathers. It is who we are now, and it is who we have always been.

My thanks to each of you for your help and support during the past year. I am truly blessed to work alongside so many talented people and in such an amazing industry.

***Happy Holidays from Sue Godschalk!***
