Federated Auto Parts is pleased to welcome California-based SSF Imported Auto Parts LLC to its growing membership of top-tier automotive parts distributors.

“We are very happy to have SSF Imported Auto Parts join the Federated family as our newest member,” said Sue Godschalk, CEO of Federated Auto Parts. “Their industry expertise and dedication to serving professional technicians makes them a valuable addition to our membership. We look forward to a strong partnership and continued growth together.”

With a long-standing focus on OE-quality replacement parts for European vehicles, [SSF](https://ssfautoparts.com/) brings valuable expertise and a strong reputation for supporting professional repair shops with the parts they need to do the job right. Their deep product knowledge, experienced team and strategically located warehouses make them a great addition to the Federated family.

“This is a great opportunity for SSF,” said Thomas Beer, CEO of SSF Imported Auto Parts. “We are looking forward to working alongside a group of like-minded partners who value quality, innovation and service. Joining Federated helps us continue doing what we do best, serving our customers with OE-quality parts and expert service and support.”

As part of Federated, SSF will have access to expanded resources, programs and technology tools, while contributing their strengths in the European vehicle segment to the group’s overall efforts.
