April is going to be a very busy month of preparation for the Federated staff.

April 17 is the Federated second quarter webinar. This webinar will concentrate heavily on how to prepare for the upcoming The Group National Meeting, which is being held April 28-May 1 at the Gaylord Resort and Conference Center in National Harbor, Maryland.

The Federated staff always strives to help members get the most value during the meetings. We want your time to be well spent and profitable.

The upcoming webinar will focus on how to make the most of your one-on-one meetings with vendors. There are many tools available to you and we will be discussing what some of those tools are.

Jason Scott will also offer some tips and updates on APSG lines.

Last but not least, we will have an information update on the DRiV lines available from our Co-Man Warehouse.

It’s going to be a great webinar with a lot of great information.

Benjamin Franklin once said, “By failing to prepare, you’re preparing to fail.”

Let the Federated staff help you to prepare to make the most of your time at the upcoming The Group National Meeting. Mark your calendars and join us on April 17 at 1:00 p.m. EDT.
