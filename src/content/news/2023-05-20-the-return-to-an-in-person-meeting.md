The Automotive Parts Services Group kicked off its annual meeting on Monday, April 25 at the Gaylord Texan Hotel in Grapevine, Texas. This was our first “in-person” meeting since Covid.

It was surprising how enthusiastically attendees wanted to return in person and the turnout was phenomenal. Getting away from daily routines, being around attendees who are experiencing similar challenges, and reconnecting with our supplier partners were all paramount for our members.

While it is always good to get back to business, we never want to underestimate the power of fun we have at these meetings. The social aspect that we are able to experience at receptions, dinners, in bars, and restaurants also allows us to engage and reconnect with each other.

After two years of being thrown curveballs and suffering from Zoom fatigue, we were finally able to get back to doing what we do best, conducting business face-to-face.

There were many highlights of this year’s meeting, but for me personally becoming a recipient of the Art Fisher Memorial Award is truly the highlight of my career. I was not only surprised but truly grateful and humbled to be in the same group of prestigious people that have been previous recipients of this award.

Another personal highlight of the meeting for me was being able to present an appreciation award to Donnie and Barbara Parker at Westwood Auto Parts in Birmingham, Alabama. Westwood became our first Federated member in 1985 and throughout the past 37 years, Donnie and Barbara have always been, and still remain, true supporters of Federated and our programs.

The theme “Meeting the Challenge” was a clear testament to our group and our members. We have developed strategies for meeting today’s challenges head-on and they help us to continue to strategize ways to ensure our future success.
