Six students have been awarded Federated Car Care scholarships for the 2021-22 academic year.

- Benjamin Briggs of Staunton, Virginia
- Taylor Butler of Lakeland, Florida
- Sophia Cassano of New Castle, Pennsylvania
- Alexander Pearl of Salem, Connecticut
- Jarred Simmons of Cape Coral, Florida
- Bailey Thede of Baileyville, Illinois

To be considered, applicants must meet the criteria outlined in the [Federated](http://www.automotivescholarships.com/scholarshiptype.cfm?type=45) section of the Automotive Aftermarket Scholarship Central website and must name Federated in the sponsor section of their completed application. The Federated Car Care scholarship program is administered by the University of the Aftermarket Foundation and as an added bonus, each application is considered for all scholarships listed on [AutomotiveScholarships.com](http://www.automotivescholarships.com/) where the qualifications are met.

“We are proud to once again present Federated Car Care scholarships to outstanding young men and women who excel in the classroom and are leaders making a difference in their communities,” said Sue Godschalk, president of Federated Auto Parts. “We congratulate the recipients and wish them well as they continue their education. We also want to thank everyone who applied for the scholarships and wishes them the best of luck in their future education endeavors.”

To learn more about Federated Car Care Scholarships or other scholarships being awarded by the University of the Aftermarket Foundation, visit [www.AutomotiveScholarships.com](http://www.automotivescholarships.com/).
