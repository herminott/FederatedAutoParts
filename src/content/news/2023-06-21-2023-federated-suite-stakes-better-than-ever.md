As June comes to a close, so does the Federated 2023 Suite Stakes promo.

Participants in the Suite Stakes have until June 30 to continue to earn entries.

This year’s participation in the Federated Suite Stakes promo has been better than ever with a total of 23 Federated members participating and over 12,500 customers having sales during the promotion which equated to 657,751 entries being earned on those sales.

Through May we have already awarded 1,600 weekly prizes which include gift cards, tools, hats, shirts, and work gloves.

Three grand prize trips have been awarded:

- Ultimate Weekend in St. Louis which includes tickets to a Cardinals baseball game and a World of Outlaws race at Federated Auto Parts Raceway
- SMP Training in Dallas, Texas
- Grote getaway in Cincinnati, Ohio

The June grand prize drawing will include:

- Vacation cruise
- Suite tickets to the 2024 Dirt Car Nationals in Florida
- Corvette members give-a-way

We would like to thank our vendor partners who participated in this year’s Suite Stakes. We greatly appreciate their continued support for this sales-driven promotion.

The Federated Suite Stakes promo just keeps getting better each year and is designed to help our members and participating vendors increase sales and grow their customer base. This year’s Suite Stakes produced 4.9 million in sales from new customers.  Stay tuned. Plans are already underway for an even larger Suite Stakes in 2024.
