leadership update

I recently came across an article that mentioned Christmas in July and how this has become an added occasion that many people like to celebrate. Christmas in July can actually be on any given day during the month, but most celebrations do occur on July 25.

According to the article, Christmas in July was first celebrated at a girl’s summer camp in 1933 and became even more popular when the movie “Christmas in July” arrived in theaters in 1940.

So why am I writing about Christmas in July in August? Earlier this week, I received a letter from Ted Sylvester, vice president of Toys for Tots, thanking Federated and its members for their contribution to the success of the Toys for Tots program in the past year.

Enclosed with the letter was a very nice “Certificate of Appreciation” which reads:

“Certificate of Appreciation presented in gratitude to 2023 National Corporate Sponsor Federated Auto Parts Distributors, Inc. for Outstanding Support of United States Marine Corps Reserve Toys for Tots.”

Sometimes I think we tend to forget that Toys for Tots not only distributes toys during the holiday season, but they continue their mission throughout the year with initiatives such as the Toys for Tots Literacy Program, the Toys for Tots Native American Program and the Toys for Tots Foster Care initiative.

So why not celebrate Christmas any time throughout the year by sending a contribution to Toys for Tots. It is extremely easy to donate. Just go to the Toys for Tots page on The Group’s website (https://www.thegroupapsg.com/toys-for-tots.html) and click on DONATE. Let’s make the season of giving to Toys for Tots a year-round holiday!
