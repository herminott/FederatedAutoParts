December 17, 2024
leadership updatenews

This was probably not on anyone’s bucket list, but holding The Group National Meeting in conjunction with AWDA/AAPEX is something that we can check off the list and move over to the “done that” side of the list.

It was an extremely busy week and well attended by members of the Federated Alliance and the Pronto Network.

There were many positive comments, such as:

  • “Having The Group National meeitng in conjunction with AWDA/AAPEX in Vegas saves me from having to attend two separate meetings.”
  • “I was able to get meetings with all of the vendors that I wanted to meet with.”
  • “It was good to be involved in industry events such as the AWDA awards ceremony and the AAPEX breakfast.”
  • “I especially liked walking the show floor and seeing all of the products that are available.”

But, there were some negative comments as well:

  • “It is too busy and there are too many things going on at one time.”
  • “I don’t like going to Vegas.”
  • “I have a hard time getting used to the time change.”
  • “Everything in Vegas is too expensive.”

Hats off to the staff at the Auto Care Association and MEMA. There could not have been a more helpful liason between the APSG and the Venetian Hotel.

Hats off to our joint venture partners, the Alliance. JC and his staff did a remarkable job of handling all of the production and coordination for the meeting, not to mention that fabulous “AfterGlow” party.

Hats off to our APSG partners from the Pronto Network. Robert and his staff always do a tremendous job making sure all of the members and vendors are taken care of.

And last (but certainly not least), hat’s off to the Federated staff who worked tirelessly to make sure our meeting came off in true Federated fashion.

So whether you are pro or con on the event, our main hope is that you found value in attending.

We look forward to seeing everyone at the next The Group National Meeting, April 28 – May 1, 2025, at the Gaylord National in National Harbor, Maryland.
