It was great seeing so many of you at AWDA and AAPEX. We had excellent meetings and everyone who we met with is very optimistic about the aftermarket business climate for 2024. It was also fun to see so many people at The Group’s annual reception. We had an outstanding turnout, which included the wonderful members of YANG. Thank you to all who attended this fun event.

Several people who are associated with Federated, including Bo Fisher, chairman of Federated Auto Parts, were honored with special awards during Industry Week and I wanted to recognize and congratulate them on their well-deserved honors.

Bo was recognized with the Northwood University Automotive Aftermarket Management Education Award.  The award is given annually to industry leaders throughout the country who have made noteworthy contributions to education, on any level, inside or outside of the industry. No one is more deserving of this honor than Bo, who has implemented the Art Fisher and Federated Car Care scholarships programs at Federated and was instrumental in Federated becoming a Diamond Lifetime Trustee of the University of the Aftermarket Foundation.

Ashlee Arnold received the Women of Excellence award from Women in Auto Care (WiAC) during AAPEX. Ashlee, who serves as vice president of Federated member Arnold Oil Company of Austin, has been an active member of the industry, serving on various committees including a term as president of YANG and she just finished her term as chair of AWDA. Everyone at Federated wants to congratulate Ashlee on this well-deserved award.

Our friend Paul Louwers at AVI and The Group Training Academy was presented with the AWDA Art Fisher Award for Excellence in Education. Paul and his team have been supplying modern and detailed training videos to the industry since 1994. He was also instrumental in the creation of The Group Training Academy, which has made a big impact with our members and their customers. In recognition of Paul’s award, scholarships in his name will be awarded to two students enrolled in the aftermarket program at Northwood University.

Another one of our friends, Jennifer Tio of Maximum Marketing, was honored twice during Industry Week. Jenny received the AWDA Pursuit of Excellence Award and was a recipient of the Women of Excellence Award from WiAC. For over 25 years, Jenny has been working with Federated and its members developing award-winning marketing, advertising and public relations on behalf of Federated. Jenny also serves as executive director of the University of the Aftermarket Foundation.

For those who might have missed it, I wanted to share this interesting article that appeared in Aftermarket Matters right before Industry Week. Entitled “Downturn in New Car Sales has ‘Significant Implications’ for Aftermarket,” the article is a recap of a Lang Aftermarket iReport that highlights a downturn in new car sales, but also focuses on the fact that with more older vehicles on the road, there will be a greater prospect for aftermarket product sales and service opportunities for the foreseeable future.

As we head into the Holiday season, I wanted to wish everyone a Happy Thanksgiving. I hope you get time to relax and spend time with your family and friends.
