September 15, 2023
leadership update

As I write this article, it is this week that we remember the thousands of people who lost their lives during the terrorist attacks on September 11, 2001. We not only pray for peace for the souls of those who passed, but we pray as well for peace for the survivors, friends, and family members of the victims and all who have carried the grief and emotional scars since that tragic day.

We are also called to remember and honor the service of the first responders, military personnel, and all who sacrificed their own safety to rescue, protect, and comfort their fellow countrymen during the attacks.

Our greatest tribute to these brave people is to never forget.

September is also the time that my team starts to prepare for AWDA and AAPEX.

We are fortunate to have so many vendors that we can choose to meet with. We can use the time at AWDA to meet with our current APSG-approved vendors and enrich our existing partnerships or we can choose to meet with new vendors and form new partnerships. AAPEX provides us with further opportunities to visit with the vendors that we were not able to schedule one-on-one meetings with and it also provides us with the opportunity to explore new vendors in a variety of different categories.

Whether it is at AWDA or AAPEX, our ultimate goal is to partner with the vendors who can help us remain a strong force in the marketplace as we compete against the major retailers.

If you do not plan to attend AWDA and AAPEX this year, I strongly encourage you to consider doing so in the future. I know you will find it is time well spent.

For those of you who do plan to attend AWDA and AAPEX, I want to remind you to please use the link below to register for the Group Reception on Tuesday, October 31st from 5:30-7:30 p.m. PT at the Palazzo pool deck.

https://apsgthegroup.wufoo.com/forms/the-group-reception-member-registration/

This is a “by invitation only” event and you must be registered to attend.

One final reminder: please continue to fight for “Right to Repair.” Our future depends on it.
