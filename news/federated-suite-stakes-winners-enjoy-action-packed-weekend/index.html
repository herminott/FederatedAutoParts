Federated Suite-Stakes Winners Enjoy Action-Packed Weekend

The first set of winners in the Federated Suite-Stakes contest enjoyed an incredible weekend in St. Louis of baseball, racing, and great food. August 16, 2021
contests promotionscorporate eventsfeaturednews

The first set of winners in the Federated Suite-Stakes contest enjoyed an incredible weekend in St. Louis of baseball, racing, and great food. The winners of the “Suite” grand prizes received an all-expense-paid trip for two to the World of Outlaws Ironman 55, including suite tickets and a bonus “Get Dirty with Kenny” dirt track driving experience.

“Our Federated Car Care Customers had an incredible weekend of fun and adventure, and I am sure it is a trip they will all remember for the rest of their lives,” said Phil Moore, senior vice president, of Federated Auto Parts. “From a Cardinals game and seeing the great sites of St. Louis to getting dirty with Kenny, our customers had a once-in-a-lifetime experience. We also want to thank the Federated membership and participating vendor partners who helped make this ‘suite trip’ possible.”

The winning customers included: Shawn and Carrie Alden; Timothy and Lisa Dinser; Chelsea and Benjamin Grither; Maurice Hendrickson and Timothy Porter; Walter Tarr and Marlin Parke; and John Weber and Sarah Hill.

The “Suite-Stakes” weekend experience included seats in a skybox for the St. Louis Cardinals-Kansas City Royals baseball game plus a tour of the Budweiser Brewery in St. Louis. The winners also attended the World of Outlaws Ironman 55 race where they watched the race from the luxury of a suite at the Federated Auto Parts Raceway in Pevely, Missouri. The weekend ended with NASCAR legend Kenny Schrader providing his unique teaching methods in a “Get Dirty with Kenny” driving experience at the raceway.

Over 11,000 Federated customers signed up to participate in the Federated Suite-Stakes, the biggest rewards program Federated Auto Parts has ever conducted for its customers. Federated awarded more than 4,500 prizes, including “Super Suite” and “Suite” grand prizes.

The winners of the “Super Suite” grand prizes will have an all-expense paid trip for two to the Federated Auto Parts 400 Salute to First Responders on September 11. The winners will watch the NASCAR playoff race in the luxury of the Federated suite at the Richmond Raceway.
