January 25, 2021
leadership update

I have officially completed my first year in the role of president of Federated Auto Parts Distributors.

The year 2020 started out on a high note with many of our members reporting sizeable increases in business. The economic forecast was favorable, but in early March, when COVID-19 entered our lives, everything changed.

The pandemic brought more challenges than any of us could have ever imagined. However, with those challenges there also came the ability to learn, to improve, and become a better leader knowing that how we were able to deal with the events brought about by the pandemic would be critical to both our employees and our business. Learning to navigate through the guidelines and adapt to the changes certainly presented challenges. If leadership is forged through experience and the capability to learn and deal with unexpected outcomes, then this year has certainly been my trial by fire.

What was the hardest part of 2020? For me, it was not being able to see family and friends in person and seeing the numbers continue to rise daily on deaths due to the pandemic; knowing that their loved ones may not have been able to be with them to offer comfort or to say goodbye.

As we enter into the new year, it is my greatest hope that the vaccine will help to bring the virus under control and that it will help protect the frontline workers and the most vulnerable and prevent them from getting this terrible virus.

Will we eventually return to things as they were prior to the pandemic? I hope that some of the more important things are restored, such as kids being able to return to the classroom, being able to be with family and friends for holiday celebrations, and being able to travel safely again. I am looking forward to the opportunity to rediscover all of the things I previously took for granted and I vow to cherish them with a new and greater appreciation.
