October 11, 2023
leadership update

October is considered the month of seasonal change. Fall is starting to kick in. The sky is looking bluer, the nights are getting cooler and the trees are beginning to show their magnificent colors.

You may love October for many reasons, and if you are a “foodie” like me, October is everything pumpkin, cinnamon and spice, apple cider and of course mulled wine.

For the automotive aftermarket, both September and October are filled with important events.

In September, some of our Federated members and staff made a trip to Capitol Hill for the Auto Care Fall Leadership Days and Legislative summit.  The Legislative summit presented a huge opportunity for in depth briefings and face- to-face meetings with members of congress and their staff. Attendees were able to discuss many concerns and issues currently facing the automotive industry including the all-important “Right to Repair” Act.

October brings the annual trip to Las Vegas for AWDA and AAPEX.  Although we start final preparations early in October for this very important meeting with our manufacturer partners, we actually prepare all year long for this event by noting things that warrant further discussion in a face-to-face setting.

I would like to encourage all Federated members to be involved in AWDA and take the time to meet with our suppliers. I think you will see that it definitely time well spent.

Also, in case you were not able to watch the recent “Celebrate National Coffee Day with industry GOATS” webinar, and by the way, GOATS is an acronym for “Greatest of All Time,” I highly suggest you watch this. Paul McCarthy served as moderator for the panel consisting of Larry Pavey, John Washbish and Bill Long. These three long time industry leaders do an excellent job in describing some of the changes and challenges they have seen. They also share a bit of how they got started in the automotive industry and what it has meant to them. To view the webinar, click here. One thing is evident, whether you have been in the automotive industry for decades or a short period, you will never fail to realize how fortunate we are to work in this great automotive aftermarket industry.
