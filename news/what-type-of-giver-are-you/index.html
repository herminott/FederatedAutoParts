February 15, 2024
leadership update

Last week I learned that a very close and dear friend of mine had recently been recognized at an event for a contribution that she had made. I was not surprised in the least that my friend had made this contribution, but the fact that she was being recognized for a gift that she had made prior to her death in 2018 for a project that was finally completed gave me pause.

My friend was a “Quiet Giver.” She was a behind the scenes giver who chose to avoid promotion or recognition. She was a kind and caring person who connected with her friends and her community by giving in the quietest of ways and I realized that the level of her generosity and acts of kindness were just like her friendship; consistent, steadfast and true.

On the other hand, there are givers who like the ceremony and they like the recognition. You also have givers who choose to contribute only during the holidays, but no matter what type of giver you choose to be, all givers are important, and I am always grateful for your generosity and compassion.

When I think about giving, the future of our industry and the University of the Aftermarket Foundation comes to mind. The University of the Aftermarket Foundation awards scholarships each year to support the continued education for the next generation of automotive professionals. In the ever-changing environment of the aftermarket having the necessary skills is so important to meet the challenges of a competitive marketplace.

I hope you will consider giving to the University of the Aftermarket Foundation. It is very easy to donate online at https://uofa-foundation.org/Donate/. It makes no difference whether you are a highly visible contributor or a quiet giver, the important thing is that you give your support to the things that you believe in and the things that will make you feel that you are a part of something special, and most importantly, the things that will make a difference.
