January 25, 2023
leadership update

Each year people around the world make New Year’s resolutions. I am no exception. I learned many years ago that you do not voice these resolutions out loud, because someone might hold you to them (like a spouse or a good friend).  Instead, I make several mental notes to myself and hope that over the next 364 days, I can accomplish at least a couple of them.

I think that most New Year’s resolutions are the same year after year with the No. 1 resolution probably being some type of health improvement, either a promise to lose weight, eat healthier, exercise more, get more sleep, etc. I gave up on that one years ago.

After the holidays, it is easier to eat healthier without all the temptations that come with family gatherings and that sweet potato casserole or that special dessert that only comes out at holiday time. But unfortunately, I have found that as time goes by, I forget that resolution and revert to old eating habits. I just try to use good judgment and do the best I can when it comes to food, and I try not to not feel too guilty when I have ice cream or a decadent dessert.

Instead of going down a path that I know I am going to eventually jump off, I try to make resolutions that I feel fairly confident that I can keep.

  • Be nicer to people. I like to think that am generally a nice person, so this one should not be that hard to keep.

  • Work harder. I tend to already be an overachiever, so this one should be pretty easy as well.

  • Reduce stress. This is one that might be a little more difficult because I am a worrier. I tend to worry about everything, even things that are sometimes beyond my control, so this one will definitely take a little more effort.

  • And last but not least, be happy. This is the most important one, because amazingly enough, if you are happy with yourself and your life in general, then you are a nicer person. If you are happy with your job, you do work harder to constantly improve and make your company the best it can be. If you are happy, life is less stressful because you understand what is important and what makes you the person you are.

So, for those of you who made resolutions, I wish you good luck and I hope you are able to carry through with them. From the entire Federated staff, we wish everyone a very Happy New Year!
