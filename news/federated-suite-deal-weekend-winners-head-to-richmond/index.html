Federated Suite Deal Weekend Winners Head to Richmond

August 30, 2022
contests promotionsfeaturednews

Two NASCAR races, go-karting with Kenny Schrader, and shuffleboard with Justin Allgaier were just some of the highlights for the grand prize winners of a special Suite Deal weekend awarded as part of the Federated Suite-Stakes program.

“The Suite Deal winners had a phenomenal time in Richmond, enjoying a fun-filled weekend of NASCAR racing and hospitality,” said Phil Moore, senior vice president for Federated. “We want to thank everyone at the Richmond Raceway for being such terrific hosts and Justin Allgaier and Kenny Schrader for providing a memorable experience for our winners.”

The Federated Suite Deal weekend kicked off with a welcome dinner. The next day, Schrader hosted the Federated Car Care customers for a morning of go-kart racing before the group headed to Richmond Raceway for the NASCAR Truck Series race. The grand finale was the Federated Auto Parts 400 where the winners enjoyed the race from a luxury suite. Kevin Harvick won the nationally televised race for the second year in a row.

The grand prize winners of the Federated Suite-Stakes promotion who traveled to Richmond, Virginia for the Suite Deal weekend were Kirt and Stephanie Koeller, Chen and Yuzhen Lu, Eric and Cayden Stroh, Vishaul and Surrigedai Persaud, Justin Pry and Blake Scylor, Bradley and Tracey Schaefer and Michael Whitacre and Isaac Bloch.

The 2022 Federated Suite-Stakes promotion was conducted for Federated customers in good standing who enrolled in the contest before June 30, 2022. Over 4,000 prizes have been awarded this year, including all-in Las Vegas getaways and pick-your-destination vacations.

“We want to thank the Federated members, vendors, and customers who participated in the 2022 Suite-Stakes promotion. We had greater participation and awarded more prizes than ever before,” concluded Moore.
