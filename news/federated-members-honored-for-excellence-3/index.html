Federated Auto Parts recently recognized four members with top honors for their outstanding commitment to the success of vital Federated initiatives.

The Federated Co-Man Member of the Year Award was presented to Wilson Automotive Warehouse of Plainfield, New Jersey for their outstanding support of the member-owned Co-Man warehouse operation and its programs.

Warehouse Inc. of Hays, Kansas was recognized with the Federated Marketing Excellence Award for demonstrating consistent excellence in their marketing activities and providing support for other members where possible.

Staunton, Virginia-based Fisher Auto Parts was honored with the National Accounts Excellence Award for excellence in serving national account customers.

Hovis Auto & Truck Supply of Mercer, Pennsylvania was named the inaugural honoree of the Federated IT Participation and Utilization Award for its outstanding leadership, adoption and use of technology in its operations.

“We deeply appreciate the continued commitment of the Federated membership to leveraging our business-building initiatives,” said Sue Godschalk, president of Federated Auto Parts. “These four outstanding members have distinguished themselves through their exceptional support of core programs. We applaud them for earning these distinguished honors.”
