February 19, 2023
leadership update

Our 2021 Group National Meeting, which was optimistically scheduled to be held at the Mirage Resort in Las Vegas April 12-16, is now going to be a virtual meeting.

Las Vegas, and therefore the Mirage, are still under the restrictions that are in place due to COVID-19. At present, the Mirage is now open only Thursday through early Monday and gatherings are limited to 50 individuals or 25 percent of fire code capacity (whichever is less). These occupancy restrictions, coupled with company travel bans that have remained in place for both members and manufacturers, were two of the deciding factors that moved us once again to the virtual format.

Over the past year, virtual meetings have become an effective and necessary means of maintaining communication. Virtual meetings allow more participants to attend without the added cost of rooms, food and beverage, and airfare. It also eliminates travel time and time spent away from businesses, which is always a concern for business owners.

While virtual meetings do provide cost savings to participants, there are still many costs involved in planning and producing a successful virtual meeting. Meeting planners and hosts spend an enormous amount of time on content and production costs can vary greatly depending on the complexity of the meeting. Finding ways to keep the audience engaged while providing valuable information, is a delicate balancing act and vital to the success of a virtual meeting.

Many predictions indicate that virtual meetings are here to stay and will be part of the new normal. I, for one, hope this prediction is wrong. I am a social person and after months of quarantine and social distancing, I am eagerly looking forward to the day when we can once again meet in person. I long to be on the receiving end of a handshake. I look forward to once again spending time with my friends, having dinner in a restaurant where we can be face to face, talking about our families, and sharing stories. I look forward to not having to wear a mask and not having to social distance. I think it would be safe to say that we all hope that day will come sooner rather than later.
