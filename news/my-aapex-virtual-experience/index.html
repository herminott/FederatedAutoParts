November 13, 2020
leadership update

November 3rd was a big day for me. (No, I was not running for public office). It was a big day because it was the opening of the first Virtual AAPEX Show and I participated in a round table discussion with four other esteemed panelists from the automotive industry. The topic of discussion: “What to Expect on the Road Ahead.”

Bill Hanvey and Paul McCarthy did an excellent job in presenting questions that anyone who is associated with the automotive industry was certainly looking to gain answers to and further insight on.

The panelists all agreed that the automotive industry has proven its resiliency over the past few months and we found that, for the most part, even though we are in different segments of the aftermarket, we all are facing the same challenges going forward. Employee and customer safety remains at the forefront of everyone’s concern, closely followed by supply chain interruption. We all agree that E-commerce has played a major role in the aftermarket since the onset of the pandemic. Consumers now opt for online ordering, not only for convenience but also due to brick-and-mortar closures and safety concerns. Going forward there will certainly be a greater focus placed on all digital resources.

As leaders in our respective companies, we all have faced very challenging times during this crisis and the road ahead is certain to be challenging as well. We cannot predict the future, but we can strive to be better prepared so that we can act accordingly to ensure our survival.
