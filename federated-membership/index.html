Federated Car Care Centers

The Federated Car Care Center Program is premiere nationwide network of independently-owned repair facilities. With thousands of locations nationwide, there’s sure to be a car care center near you. Federated Car Care Centers can take care of all of your preventative maintenance, diagnostic and major repair needs.

You can trust your car to the Federated Car Care Professional. Federated Car Care Centers employ highly qualified, ASE certified technicians for all repair services and install brand name, quality parts that meet or exceed OE specifications. Federated Car Care Centers are also well trained in the latest repair and diagnostic procedures and maintain state-of-the-art equipment to diagnose and repair the latest models.
