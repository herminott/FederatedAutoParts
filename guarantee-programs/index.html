Repair Warranty

In today’s aggressive marketplace it is difficult and expensive to acquire new customers. Why take the chance that a re-repair and the frustrations that accompany it will drive your customer to your competition? The Nationwide Repair Warranty will help you and your customers have peace of mind.

Your customers will appreciate knowing that they will have a warranty on the repairs performed no matter where they are, and you will be at ease knowing that if a problem does arise for them, it can easily be taken care of. Federated Auto Parts has a network of associated facilities, which offer warranted repairs, to help the customer in their time of need. We will locate the closest facility for your customers and assist them with getting their vehicle in for an evaluation.

Roadside Assistance

The Federated Roadside Assistance Program is valid up to or for 365 days after a customer receives a service or repair on their automobile or light truck. Eligible consumers who have qualified for Free Roadside Assistance and are in need of roadside assistance must call 1-800-351-8438 to be connected with the nationwide service provider.

  • The driver of the vehicle must be with the vehicle when the service provider arrives, as roadside assistance cannot be provided to an unattended vehicle.
  • The consumer pays for the requested service. The consumer will be reimbursed for covered services up to a maximum of $125.00 per occurrence. The service provider requires use of a credit card to arrange for dispatch.

Labor Assurance

The Federated Labor Assurance Program covers labor reimbursement inside the 25-mile restriction of the Nationwide Repair Warranty Program. This program provides reimbursement for labor on most repairs involving defective parts originally installed by your Federated Car Care Center.

Claims are administered by Automotive Business Solutions, the provider of our Nationwide Repair Warranty, Roadside Assistance, and Road Hazard Programs. Your shop must be enrolled in both the Federated Car Care and Nationwide Repair Warranty programs to eligible to participate.
