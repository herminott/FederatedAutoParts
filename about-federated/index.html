About Federated

Meet the Leader

Program distribution groups come in many sizes and varieties, and Federated Auto Parts Distributors is fueled by exemplary vision and leadership and driven by aggressive service and top-quality name-brand parts. Founded in 1985 by Art Fisher and Rusty Bishop of Fisher Auto Parts, a Virginia-based traditional automotive store chain with more than 400 locations.

By having a substantial number of company-owned stores and dependable distribution within the association, Federated has made line commonality a reality. Federated has been able to establish long-term partnerships with vendors.
Federated provides a vast array of marketing programs designed to meet the needs of all of our members. Federated encourages the utilization of national advertising and promotional programs which have been specifically designed to tie both dealer and installer customers to the Federated jobber.

In addition to our strong vendor relationships and our exemplary marketing programs, Federated Co-Man Warehouse provides added value exclusively to Federated members as a single supply source for a variety of automotive products.

Federated’s primary goal has always been to exceed customer expectations. To meet this goal Federated continues to supply the highest quality parts from today’s most respected suppliers. We invite you to learn more about the group everyone is talking about and to join Federated Auto Parts Distributors!

Our History

Federated Auto Parts was founded in 1985 and is headquartered in Staunton, Virginia. The goal of the cooperative was to pool resources and purchasing power to better compete with large, national chains.

Over the next few decades, Federated Auto Parts grew rapidly through acquisitions and partnerships. In 1998, the company merged with the Automotive Distribution Network, creating one of the largest automotive parts distributors in North America.
